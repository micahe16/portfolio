Evacuation Drill App and Dashboard

WebstaurantStore · Operations Data Analyst Internship

The Project

I worked on a way to record evacuation drill times and make the results easier for the safety team to review. The project combined a Power Apps app, a SharePoint List, and a Power BI dashboard.

What I Built

The app records times for 11 zones, along with the drill type, date and time, evacuation controller, and shift. Drill records are stored in SharePoint and used in Power BI.

The dashboard includes average, maximum, and minimum times, comparisons between drills, and a callout for the slowest zone. These views help the team see where to focus follow-up.

How the Pieces Connect

  1. Record drill information and zone times in Power Apps.
  2. Save the records to a SharePoint List.
  3. Review and compare results in Power BI.

Tools

Power Apps, SharePoint, and Power BI.
