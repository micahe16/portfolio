Database Platform Comparison

PELSA Company · BUAD 346 academic project

The Project

I compared options for upgrading PELSA’s project database from Google Sheets. The goal was a system with a simple employee interface, different access levels, and manageable upkeep.

My Approach

I evaluated PostgreSQL with NocoDB, Grist, and Airtable using a weighted scoring model and the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP). I also considered how changes in cost, setup difficulty, and maintenance could affect the decision.

Recommendation

Grist ranked highest in both analyses. I recommended it as a balance between customization and ease of use. This presentation describes the evaluation and recommendation, rather than a completed implementation.
